Summary

Outfitly stores your wardrobe only on your device. The optional Community feature lets you publish selected photos to other members; only what you choose to share is uploaded. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

Clothing photos, item details, outfits, calendar plans, wear history and app preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Colour detection, duplicate detection, background removal for the try-on avatar (Google ML Kit, on-device) and outfit suggestions run entirely on your device. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Clear all data.

Data that leaves your device

  • Community (optional, only if you create an account): your email address and password (managed by our hosting provider Supabase, servers in Singapore), your username, display name and bio, the photos, titles and captions you choose to publish, and likes, view counts, reports and blocks. Published posts are visible to other community members. You can delete any post, make your profile private, or delete your community account in the app (Community → account → Delete community account), which permanently removes your profile, posts, photos and likes from our servers. To request deletion without the app, email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].
  • When you choose to share: sharing an outfit image or exporting a backup file sends that file only to the app or location you select.
  • Advertising: the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address and limited device information to serve, personalise (where you consent) and measure ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent and can change their choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Permissions

Internet access is used for ads and, if you use it, the Community feature. Outfitly does not request camera or storage permissions: photos are taken with your camera app and chosen through the Android photo picker.

Children

Outfitly is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
