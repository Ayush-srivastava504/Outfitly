# Play Store listing — Outfitly

**App name (30):** Outfitly – Wardrobe & Outfits

**Short description (80):**
Organise your closet, build outfits and get daily outfit ideas. Private & offline.

**Full description:**

Outfitly turns your closet into a beautiful, searchable digital wardrobe — and helps you decide what to wear every day.

👕 YOUR WARDROBE, ORGANISED
• Add clothes from your camera or gallery — even several photos at once
• Colour is detected automatically on your phone
• Tag category, type, season, occasion, style and brand
• Search, filter and sort in a grid or list
• Get a heads-up when you add something that looks like a duplicate

✨ OUTFIT IDEAS THAT MAKE SENSE
• Pick today's weather and the occasion — Outfitly suggests complete outfits from your own clothes
• Every suggestion explains why it works: colour harmony, style, favourites
• Avoids repeating what you wore recently and nudges forgotten pieces back into rotation

🧩 OUTFIT BUILDER
• Combine tops, bottoms, dresses, outerwear, shoes, accessories and bags
• Save, edit, duplicate and favourite your looks
• Share an outfit as an image

📅 PLAN & TRACK
• Plan outfits on a monthly or weekly calendar
• Mark what you wore and build a wear history

📊 WARDROBE INSIGHTS
• Category and colour breakdowns
• Most- and least-worn pieces, never-worn items and favourite combinations

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up
• Your photos never leave your phone — all processing happens on-device
• Works fully offline
• Export a backup file whenever you like

Outfitly is free and supported by ads. Weather is chosen by you — the app doesn't track your location.
